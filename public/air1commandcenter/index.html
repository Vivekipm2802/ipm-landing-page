How to Apply — Step-by-Step (IPMAT 2026)

1

Register on the Official NTA Portal

Go to ipmat.iimindore.ac.in or jipmat.nta.ac.in. Create an account with your email and mobile number. Keep these credentials safe — you'll need them until final admission.

2

Fill in Personal & Academic Details

Enter Class 10 and Class 12 marks accurately. If you are appearing in Class 12 in 2026, enter provisional marks. You must clear 60% at the time of admission.

3

Upload Documents

Passport-size photograph (white background, recent), signature (black ink on white paper), and Class 10 certificate. All uploads must meet the exact size specifications on the form.

4

Choose Exam Centre

Select your preferred exam city. Popular centres fill up fast — select your city and a backup city. Centres are allocated on a first-come-first-served basis.

5

Pay the Application Fee & Submit

Pay via Net Banking / Credit / Debit Card / UPI. Download and save the confirmation page. The fee is non-refundable. Application not complete until fee payment is confirmed.

Frequently Asked Questions — IPM & IPMAT Forms 2026

When do IPM and IPMAT application forms open for 2026?

IPMAT Indore forms typically open in March and close in early May. JIPMAT opens slightly later, usually in April. Private institutes like Ashoka and TAPMI open forms as early as January. Check the master table above for current status — it is updated regularly.

Can I apply to multiple IPM programs with one application?

No. Each institute has a separate application portal and fee. However, your IPMAT Indore score is accepted by IIM Indore, IIM Shillong, IIM Sambalpur, Ashoka, and IIM Kozhikode — one exam, multiple applications. Apply to each institute individually using your IPMAT scorecard.

What is the application fee for IPMAT 2026?

IPMAT Indore charges ₹4,000 for General/EWS/OBC and ₹2,000 for SC/ST/PwD candidates. JIPMAT has a similar fee structure. Private institute fees vary from ₹1,000 to ₹3,000. All fees are non-refundable. Applying to 5–6 programs costs ₹10,000–₹20,000 total — worth it for the optionality.

Can students appearing in Class 12 in 2026 apply?

Yes. Students currently in Class 12 (2025–26 batch) are fully eligible to apply for IPMAT, JIPMAT, and all IPM programs. You enter your predicted or provisional marks. The actual 60% eligibility is verified at the time of admission — not application.

Is there an age limit for IPM 2026?

IIM Indore's IPM has a maximum age of 20 years for General/OBC/EWS on July 31 of the admission year. SC/ST/PwD candidates get 22 years. IIM Rohtak and JIPMAT have similar limits. Private institutes like TAPMI and Ashoka have no strict age limit.

What happens if I miss the IPMAT form deadline?

There is no late submission window. Missing the deadline means you cannot sit the 2026 exam — the next opportunity is IPMAT 2027. This is why applying early (ideally within the first 2 weeks of the form opening) is strongly recommended. There is no advantage to waiting.

Does stream or subject combination in Class 12 matter for IPM?

No. There is no stream restriction. Science, Commerce, and Arts students are all eligible for IPMAT and IPM programs. The exam tests Quantitative Ability and Verbal Ability — both stream-neutral. Students from non-Math backgrounds can and do crack IPMAT with focused preparation.

The Bottom Line

The IPM and IPMAT application process is straightforward — but unforgiving on deadlines. With most forms closing in April and May 2026, the window is short. The students who crack IIM Indore IPM are rarely more talented than those who miss out; they are simply more organised.

Apply early, apply to multiple programs, and then put all your energy into preparation. The form is the ticket — the exam is the real game.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the expected IPMAT Indore cutoff for General category in 2026?

Based on 2022–2025 trends, the expected overall cutoff for IIM Indore IPM (General category) is 200–230 out of 400. However, sectional cutoffs apply independently — you must clear QA MCQ, QA SA and VA cutoffs separately. All figures are indicative; verify against official IIM Indore results.

Is there a sectional cutoff in IPMAT 2026?

Yes, and this is the most common reason candidates fail to convert despite a good overall score. IPMAT Indore has independent sectional cutoffs for Quantitative Ability (MCQ), Quantitative Ability (Short Answer) and Verbal Ability. Clearing all three is mandatory.

What is the IPMAT cutoff for OBC, SC and ST category students?

Category-wise cutoffs are typically 10–20% lower than General category. OBC candidates generally need 5–15 marks less, SC/ST candidates 25–40 marks less than the General cutoff. The exact relaxation varies by IIM and year. Check the live cutoff predictor above for current estimates.

How is the IPMAT Indore cutoff calculated each year?

The cutoff is set after normalisation based on the difficulty of that year's paper and the number of candidates appearing. A harder paper results in a lower cutoff. IIM Indore announces the cutoff along with WAT-PI shortlist results, typically in June.

What is a safe score for IIM Rohtak IPM 2026?

IIM Rohtak conducts IPMAT Rohtak (separate from IPMAT Indore). Based on previous years, a total score of 65–75+ out of 160 is considered safe for the General category shortlist. Rohtak also has sectional cutoffs across its three sections.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the age limit for IPMAT 2026?

For IPMAT Indore 2026, candidates must have been born on or after August 1, 2004 (General/EWS/OBC) or August 1, 2002 (SC/ST/PwD). This means General category candidates must be 20 years old or younger at the time of admission. Use the eligibility checker above to verify your DOB instantly.

What is the minimum percentage required for IPMAT eligibility?

IPMAT Indore requires a minimum of 60% in both Class 10 and Class 12 for General/EWS/OBC candidates, and 55% for SC/ST/PwD candidates. IIM Rohtak requires 60% in Class 12 (50% for SC/ST). Candidates appearing in Class 12 in 2026 are also eligible to apply.

Is Mathematics compulsory for IPMAT eligibility?

Mathematics is NOT compulsory for IPMAT Indore eligibility — students from any stream (Science, Commerce, Arts/Humanities) can apply. However, the exam has a heavy Quantitative Ability section, so students with a Math background have a natural advantage. IIM Rohtak also does not mandate Math.

Can a student currently in Class 12 apply for IPMAT 2026?

Yes. Students appearing in their Class 12 board exams in 2026 are eligible to apply for IPMAT 2026, provided they meet the age criteria. However, admission will be conditional on scoring the required percentage in the final board results.

How many attempts are allowed for IPMAT?

IIM Indore IPM allows a maximum of 2 attempts for General/EWS/OBC candidates and 3 attempts for SC/ST/PwD. IIM Rohtak allows up to 3 attempts for all categories. Each year's exam counts as one attempt, so plan carefully if you are considering a repeat year.

What is the eligibility for JIPMAT 2026 (IIM Jammu and Bodh Gaya)?

JIPMAT 2026 requires candidates to have passed or be appearing in Class 12 with at least 60% marks (55% for SC/ST). There is no separate age limit for JIPMAT. Both IIM Jammu and IIM Bodh Gaya accept JIPMAT scores for their IPM programs.

Frequently Asked Questions

What formulas are most important for IPMAT Quantitative Ability?

The highest-weightage QA topics in IPMAT are: Number Theory (HCF, LCM, divisibility, remainders), Arithmetic (percentages, profit-loss, SI/CI, ratio-proportion), Algebra (linear equations, quadratics, progressions), Geometry (triangles, circles, mensuration) and Modern Maths (permutation-combination, probability). The cheat sheets above cover all of these in compact format.

How should I use IPMAT cheat sheets in the last week before the exam?

In the final 7 days: Day 1–2: review formula sheets and attempt 1 full PYQ. Day 3–4: topic-wise revision of weak areas using cheat sheets. Day 5: 1 full mock test under timed conditions. Day 6: review wrong answers, re-read cheat sheets. Day 7: light revision only, no new concepts.

What verbal topics should I revise using the IPMAT cheat sheets?

Focus on: Reading Comprehension (inference-based questions), Para-jumbles, Sentence Correction (subject-verb agreement, tense errors), Vocabulary in context, and Critical Reasoning. The VA cheat sheet above covers rules, common error patterns and RC strategies in one quick-reference format.

Are these cheat sheets useful for JIPMAT and IPMAT Rohtak too?

Yes. The QA cheat sheets apply to all three exams since the syllabus overlaps significantly. The VA cheat sheet is most tailored to IPMAT Indore, but the core grammar rules and reading comprehension strategies work for JIPMAT and Rohtak as well.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the study strategy of IPMAT AIR 1 holders?

Common patterns across AIR 1 holders: (1) Solve 5+ years of PYQs thoroughly, (2) Prioritise accuracy over speed in the first 3 months, (3) Take 1 full mock per week in the final 2 months, (4) Spend 70% of QA time on Number Theory and Algebra, (5) Read a quality newspaper daily for VA and current affairs for WAT.

How many hours do IPMAT toppers study per day?

Most toppers report 4–6 focused hours per day during the peak preparation phase (3–4 months before the exam), with 2–3 hours daily in the initial phase. Quality of practice matters more than hours — 4 hours of deliberate practice with analysis outperforms 8 hours of passive reading.

What books and resources did IPMAT toppers use?

Most toppers recommend: QA — Arun Sharma (CAT level, not all chapters needed), NCERT Math 9–12, PYQs. VA — Word Power Made Easy (Norman Lewis), any standard grammar book, daily newspaper reading. For mocks — official IPMAT mock and coaching-specific mocks. Avoid using too many books simultaneously.

Can I crack IPMAT in 6 months of preparation?

6 months is sufficient for most candidates if preparation is structured and consistent. Students who struggle with Math may need 8–12 months. The key milestones: Month 1–2: concept building, Month 3–4: topic-wise practice, Month 5: full mocks and analysis, Month 6: revision and PYQ solving.

How do IPMAT toppers manage QA and VA preparation together?

The recommended split is 60% QA and 40% VA for most candidates, especially those from non-Math backgrounds. Commerce and Arts students often need to invert this to 70% QA and 30% VA to close the Math gap. VA can be improved faster; QA requires sustained practice over months.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is life like at IIM Indore IPM?

IIM Indore IPM is a 5-year integrated programme combining BBA (3 years) and MBA (2 years). The first two years focus on foundation courses, Years 3–4 on core management, and Year 5 on specialisation and placements. Campus life is intense — club activities, case competitions, live projects and a diverse peer group make it different from a regular BBA.

How are IIM Indore IPM students placed after completing the programme?

IPM students graduate with an MBA degree from IIM Indore, which means they access the same placement process as the flagship PGP (MBA) batch. Average placements are in the ₹25–30 LPA range, with top roles in consulting (McKinsey, BCG, Bain), finance (Goldman Sachs, JP Morgan) and product management.

Is the IIM Indore IPM worth it compared to a regular BBA + MBA?

For students certain about management careers, IPM offers a clear advantage: IIM brand from age 18, access to IIM alumni networks, top placement outcomes and a structured 5-year journey. The opportunity cost is locking in at 17–18 without exploring other fields. Students who later discover different interests sometimes find this limiting.

What do IPM students say about the workload and pressure?

The programme is academically rigorous. First-year students often describe the workload as a significant adjustment from school — heavy reading lists, assignments, group projects and exams run simultaneously. By Year 3, most students feel more comfortable managing the load. The peer group quality is consistently cited as the biggest advantage.

How is campus life at IIM Rohtak IPM different from IIM Indore?

IIM Rohtak's IPM is a newer programme (started 2019) with a smaller batch size. Campus infrastructure is still maturing compared to IIM Indore's established campus. However, IIM Rohtak offers the same IIM-affiliation advantage, and placements have been improving year-on-year with average packages in the ₹12–18 LPA range.
