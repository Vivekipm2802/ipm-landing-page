IIM Indore IPMAT 2026 — Application Details

IIM Indore's IPM (Integrated Programme in Management) is the most sought-after 5-year BBA+MBA programme in India. The application is made through NTA, and the admit card / score is then used to apply to IIM Indore directly. The two steps are separate — missing either means disqualification.

JIPMAT 2026 — IIM Jammu & IIM Bodhgaya

JIPMAT (Joint Integrated Programme in Management Aptitude Test) is conducted by NTA for admission to IIM Jammu and IIM Bodhgaya's 5-year IPM programmes. A single application and exam score is used for both IIMs. It is a separate exam from IPMAT Indore — aspirants often apply to both.

How to Apply — Step-by-Step (IPMAT 2026)

1

Register on the Official NTA Portal

Go to ipmat.iimindore.ac.in or jipmat.nta.ac.in. Create an account with your email and mobile number. Keep these credentials safe — you'll need them until final admission.

2

Fill in Personal & Academic Details

Enter Class 10 and Class 12 marks accurately. If you are appearing in Class 12 in 2026, enter provisional marks. You must clear 60% at the time of admission.

3

Upload Documents

Passport-size photograph (white background, recent), signature (black ink on white paper), and Class 10 certificate. All uploads must meet the exact size specifications on the form.

4

Choose Exam Centre

Select your preferred exam city. Popular centres fill up fast — select your city and a backup city. Centres are allocated on a first-come-first-served basis.

5

Pay the Application Fee & Submit

Pay via Net Banking / Credit / Debit Card / UPI. Download and save the confirmation page. The fee is non-refundable. Application not complete until fee payment is confirmed.

Frequently Asked Questions — IPM & IPMAT Forms 2026

When do IPM and IPMAT application forms open for 2026?

IPMAT Indore forms typically open in March and close in early May. JIPMAT opens slightly later, usually in April. Private institutes like Ashoka and TAPMI open forms as early as January. Check the master table above for current status — it is updated regularly.

Can I apply to multiple IPM programs with one application?

No. Each institute has a separate application portal and fee. However, your IPMAT Indore score is accepted by IIM Indore, IIM Shillong, IIM Sambalpur, Ashoka, and IIM Kozhikode — one exam, multiple applications. Apply to each institute individually using your IPMAT scorecard.

What is the application fee for IPMAT 2026?

IPMAT Indore charges ₹4,000 for General/EWS/OBC and ₹2,000 for SC/ST/PwD candidates. JIPMAT has a similar fee structure. Private institute fees vary from ₹1,000 to ₹3,000. All fees are non-refundable. Applying to 5–6 programs costs ₹10,000–₹20,000 total — worth it for the optionality.

Can students appearing in Class 12 in 2026 apply?

Yes. Students currently in Class 12 (2025–26 batch) are fully eligible to apply for IPMAT, JIPMAT, and all IPM programs. You enter your predicted or provisional marks. The actual 60% eligibility is verified at the time of admission — not application.

Is there an age limit for IPM 2026?

IIM Indore's IPM has a maximum age of 20 years for General/OBC/EWS on July 31 of the admission year. SC/ST/PwD candidates get 22 years. IIM Rohtak and JIPMAT have similar limits. Private institutes like TAPMI and Ashoka have no strict age limit.

What happens if I miss the IPMAT form deadline?

There is no late submission window. Missing the deadline means you cannot sit the 2026 exam — the next opportunity is IPMAT 2027. This is why applying early (ideally within the first 2 weeks of the form opening) is strongly recommended. There is no advantage to waiting.

Does stream or subject combination in Class 12 matter for IPM?

No. There is no stream restriction. Science, Commerce, and Arts students are all eligible for IPMAT and IPM programs. The exam tests Quantitative Ability and Verbal Ability — both stream-neutral. Students from non-Math backgrounds can and do crack IPMAT with focused preparation.

The Bottom Line

The IPM and IPMAT application process is straightforward — but unforgiving on deadlines. With most forms closing in April and May 2026, the window is short. The students who crack IIM Indore IPM are rarely more talented than those who miss out; they are simply more organised.

Apply early, apply to multiple programs, and then put all your energy into preparation. The form is the ticket — the exam is the real game.
