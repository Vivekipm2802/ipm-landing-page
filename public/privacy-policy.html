Privacy Policy

IPM Careers · Last updated: 25 October 2026

IPM Careers ("we", "us") helps students prepare for IPMAT, JIPMAT, IIM B UGAT and related exams. This page explains what information we collect through our websites (ipmcareer.com and register.ipmcareer.com), our student portal and our social media channels, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Details you give us, for example when you fill in a registration, enquiry or test form: your name, phone number, email address, city, class and the exam you are preparing for, and any answers or scores you submit.
  • Payment details when you buy a course or service. Payments are handled by our payment provider; we do not store your card or bank details.
  • Usage information such as pages visited and device or browser type, collected through cookies and analytics tools.

How we use it

  • To contact you about the courses, tests, events or guidance you asked about, by phone, email or WhatsApp.
  • To run our courses, tests and student portal, and to show you your results and reports.
  • To improve our website and content, and to measure how well our ads work.

Cookies and analytics

Our websites use cookies and similar tools from Google (Analytics, Tag Manager, Ads), Meta (Facebook Pixel) and Vercel Analytics. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us run IPM Careers (for example hosting, database, email, messaging, payment and customer management tools), and only for the purposes above, or when the law requires it.

YouTube API Services

We use YouTube API Services through our own internal tool to manage the official IPM Careers YouTube channel: uploading our videos and reading and replying to comments on our videos. This tool signs in only to the IPM Careers channel. It does not access, collect or store data from any other user's Google or YouTube account. Public comments on our videos are read only to reply to them.

By using our YouTube channel you are also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. You can review or remove app access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

How long we keep information

We keep your information only as long as we need it for the purposes above, or as long as the law requires.

Your choices

You can ask us to show, correct or delete your personal information, or to stop contacting you, by writing to the email below.

Children

Many of our students are under 18. If you are under 18, please use our services with the knowledge of a parent or guardian.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
